# Credits

## 3D Watch Case Design
**Designer:** bonybear  
**Source:** Thingiverse  
**Link:** https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7038776  
**Files used:**  
- prizma_case_bottom.stl — back cover with battery cavity  
- prizma_case_top.stl — front cover with display opening  

**Why we used this design:**  
Designed specifically for the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.28.  
Includes battery cavity, USB-C slot, and 20mm watch strap lugs.  
Lishan found this after searching Thingiverse and Printables  
when 7 local 3D printing vendors asked for STL files.  

**License:** As shared on Thingiverse by bonybear.  
Thank you bonybear for sharing your work freely.  
This design made Lishan's wearable possible.
